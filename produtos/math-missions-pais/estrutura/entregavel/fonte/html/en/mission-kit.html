Agent badges

Hand them out before the first mission. Print, cut along the dashed line, and thread a string through the hole. Your child draws their own face and makes up a secret code. When they complete a level, they color in the circle. There are 4 badges per sheet: one for each sibling (and one for the adult, if you want to play along).
