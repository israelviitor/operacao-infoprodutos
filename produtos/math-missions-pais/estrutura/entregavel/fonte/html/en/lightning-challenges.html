30 Lightning Challenges

One mini-problem a day, in 5 minutes, to practice drawing before solving on days without a mission.

How to use (for the adult)
  • Print the sheets and, if you want, cut out one challenge per day. It fits in before dinner, in the car, or in the waiting room.
  • Follow the order for your child's level: challenges 1 to 10 · Level 1, 11 to 20 · Level 2, 21 to 30 · Level 3. Start with challenge 1, even if your child is at Level 2 or 3: the first ones take 1 minute and lock in the drawing. If it gets easy, skip to the first challenge of their level.
  • There's only one rule: first the drawing, then the math. If your child gets stuck, ask “What does the question want to know?” and “Show me that in a bar.”
  • The answer key, with the expected drawing for each challenge, is on the last pages. Any drawing with the same parts counts.
  • Challenge done? Your child colors in the day's circle on the Mastery Map.
