The Secret of the Vegetable Garden Beds

Time: 25 to 30 minutes · Practice: fraction of a quantity (2/3 of 24, 3/4 of 40) · Materials: pencil, eraser, and this printed sheet (optional: analog clock, dried beans, or bottle caps).

The method in this mission: draw before you solve

Stage 2 is the “draw the problem” stage. In it, your child goes through the 3 steps of the Singapore method (details in the Parent Guide):

1. Concrete
Make 24 little paper balls (the beds) and split them into 3 equal piles. In stage 2, your child puts together 2 of the 3 piles before drawing.
2. Drawing
Your child draws the bar and puts a ? on what they want to find out. Only then do they do the math.
3. Calculation
With the drawing done, they write the equation and check the answer by looking at the drawing.

How to run the mission

  1. Read the opening out loud, with an air of mystery. Stage 1 is very short, on purpose: it gets your child into the game quickly.
  2. Don't say which operation to use. The problem doesn't say whether to add, subtract, or multiply. Figuring that out is the exercise, and the drawing is what shows the answer.
  3. If your child gets stuck, ask for the drawing before giving any help: “Show me that in a bar.” Then use the support question from the answer key (last page). It helps without giving away the answer.
  4. Each answer becomes a letter. If the code word doesn't make sense, one of the calculations is wrong: go back to the drawing and look for it together.
  5. Wrap up by asking: “Explain your drawing from stage 2 to me.”
Before moving on: You can move on to the next mission when your child explains the drawing in stage 2 in their own words (“I split it into 3 and colored in 2”). If not yet, redo just that stage another day with different numbers (e.g., 3/4 of 20).

With siblings

Print the mission pages once and solve them together, switching roles at each stage: reader (reads out loud), illustrator (draws the bar), and number cruncher (does the math and checks it against the drawing). With 2 children, the reader also draws. If they're at different levels, each one does the mission for their level and, at the end, they tell each other how they drew it.

Answer key: The Secret of the Vegetable Garden Beds

Stage 1 — The beds · 24 (letter H)
How to think: There are 3 equal rows of 8: 8 + 8 + 8. Calculation: 3 × 8 = 24.
If they get stuck, ask: “How many beds are in one row? And in three?”
Most common mistake: answering 11, when your child added 3 + 8 instead of putting together 3 rows of 8. That answer leads to a different letter, and the word doesn't work out.
Stage 2 — The watered beds · 16 (letter O)
How to think: Split into 3 equal parts (the “bottom”) and take 2 of them (the “top”). Calculation: 24 ÷ 3 = 8 · 2 × 8 = 16.
The expected drawing (any drawing with the same parts counts):
Each third is worth 24 ÷ 3 = 8. The 2 colored pieces together are worth 16.
If they get stuck, ask: “How much is 1 of the 3 pieces worth? And 2 pieces?”
Most common mistake: answering 8, when your child found only 1/3 and forgot to take 2 pieces. That answer leads to a different letter, and the word doesn't work out.
Stage 3 — The water that leaked · 30 L (letter L)
How to think: Split 40 into 4 parts (10 each) and take 3 of them. Calculation: 40 ÷ 4 = 10 · 3 × 10 = 30 L.
If they get stuck, ask: “What is 1/4 of 40? And 3/4?”
Most common mistake: answering 10 L, when your child found only 1/4 and forgot to take 3 pieces. That answer leads to a different letter, and the word doesn't work out.
Stage 4 — The leak per meter · 2 L (letter E)
How to think: The 30 L are shared equally across the 15 meters: 30 ÷ 15. Calculation: 30 ÷ 15 = 2 L per meter.
If they get stuck, ask: “If it were 30 L over 3 meters, how much per meter? And over 15?”
Most common mistake: answering 45 L, when your child added the liters to the meters. That answer leads to a different letter, and the word doesn't work out.
Code word: HOLE. The watering can had a HOLE and lost 2 L every meter: it reached the end of the garden almost empty, and only 16 beds were watered.
