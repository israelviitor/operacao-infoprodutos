The Secret of Friday Pizza Night

Time: 20 to 25 minutes · Practice: unit fractions: half, third, and fourth · Materials: pencil, eraser, and this printed sheet (optional: analog clock, dried beans, or bottle caps).

The method in this mission: draw before you solve

Stage 2 is the “draw the problem” stage. In it, your child goes through the 3 steps of the Singapore method (details in the Parent Guide):

1. Concrete
Draw a pizza on paper and cut it into 8 slices. In stage 2, your child sorts 12 dried beans (the garlic rolls) into 4 equal little piles before drawing.
2. Drawing
Your child draws the bar and puts a ? on what they want to find out. Only then do they do the math.
3. Calculation
With the drawing done, they write the equation and check the answer by looking at the drawing.

How to run the mission

  1. Read the opening out loud, with an air of mystery. Stage 1 is very short, on purpose: it gets your child into the game quickly.
  2. Don't say which operation to use. The problem doesn't say whether to add, subtract, or multiply. Figuring that out is the exercise, and the drawing is what shows the answer.
  3. If your child gets stuck, ask for the drawing before giving any help: “Show me that in a bar.” Then use the support question from the answer key (last page). It helps without giving away the answer.
  4. Each answer becomes a letter. If the code word doesn't make sense, one of the calculations is wrong: go back to the drawing and look for it together.
  5. Wrap up by asking: “Explain your drawing from stage 2 to me.”
Before moving on: You can move on to the next mission when your child explains the drawing in stage 2 in their own words (“the bar has 4 equal pieces, and I colored in 1”). If not yet, redo just that stage another day with different numbers (e.g., a fourth of 20).

With siblings

Print the mission pages once and solve them together, switching roles at each stage: reader (reads out loud), illustrator (draws the bar), and number cruncher (does the math and checks it against the drawing). With 2 children, the reader also draws. If they're at different levels, each one does the mission for their level and, at the end, they tell each other how they drew it.

Answer key: The Secret of Friday Pizza Night

Stage 1 — Half cheese · 4 (letter T)
How to think: Half is dividing into 2 equal parts: 8 ÷ 2. Calculation: 8 ÷ 2 = 4.
If they get stuck, ask: “If the pizza were divided into 2 equal sides, how many slices would be on each side?”
Most common mistake: answering 6, when your child took away 2 instead of dividing into 2 equal parts. That answer leads to a different letter, and the word doesn't work out.
Stage 2 — The garlic rolls · 3 (letter O)
How to think: A fourth is 1 of the 4 equal parts: 12 ÷ 4. Calculation: 12 ÷ 4 = 3.
The expected drawing (any drawing with the same parts counts):
A fourth is 1 of the 4 equal pieces. To find how much one piece is worth: 12 ÷ 4.
If they get stuck, ask: “Divide the 12 rolls onto 4 equal plates. How many are on each plate?”
Most common mistake: answering 8, when your child took away 4 instead of dividing into 4 equal parts. That answer leads to a different letter, and the word doesn't work out.
Stage 3 — The missing slice · 1 (letter B)
How to think: There were 4 and there are 3. What's missing is 4 − 3. Calculation: 4 − 3 = 1.
If they get stuck, ask: “From 3 to 4, how many are missing?”
Most common mistake: answering 7, when your child added instead of seeing how many are missing. That answer leads to a different letter, and the word doesn't work out.
Stage 4 — Who was in the kitchen · 2 (letter I)
How to think: A third is 1 of the 3 equal parts: 6 ÷ 3. Calculation: 6 ÷ 3 = 2.
If they get stuck, ask: “Divide the 6 members of the household into 3 equal groups. How many are in one group?”
Most common mistake: answering 9, when your child added 6 + 3 instead of dividing into 3 parts. That answer leads to a different letter, and the word doesn't work out.
Code word: TOBI. There were 2 in the kitchen: Dad and… TOBI! The dog took the cheese slice from the box while Dad went to get the plates.
