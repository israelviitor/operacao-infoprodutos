The Locked Safe Mystery

Time: 25 to 35 minutes · Practice: before and after: what changes and what stays the same · Materials: pencil, eraser, and this printed sheet (optional: analog clock, dried beans, or bottle caps).

The method in this mission: draw before you solve

Stage 2 is the “draw the problem” stage. In it, your child goes through the 3 steps of the Singapore method (details in the Parent Guide):

1. Concrete
Make two rows of bottle caps (one cap = 10 points): 35 for Nina and 21 for Caio. In stage 2, your child moves caps from one row to the other until they're equal, before drawing.
2. Drawing
Your child draws the bar and puts a ? on what they want to find out. Only then do they do the math.
3. Calculation
With the drawing done, they write the equation and check the answer by looking at the drawing.

How to run the mission

  1. Read the opening out loud, with an air of mystery. Stage 1 is very short, on purpose: it gets your child into the game quickly.
  2. Don't say which operation to use. The problem doesn't say whether to add, subtract, or multiply. Figuring that out is the exercise, and the drawing is what shows the answer.
  3. If your child gets stuck, ask for the drawing before giving any help: “Show me that in a bar.” Then use the support question from the answer key (last page). It helps without giving away the answer.
  4. Each answer becomes a letter. If the code word doesn't make sense, one of the calculations is wrong: go back to the drawing and look for it together.
  5. Wrap up by asking: “Explain your drawing from stage 2 to me.”
Before moving on: You can move on to the next mission when your child explains the drawing in stage 2 in their own words (“the difference was split in half”). If not yet, redo just that stage another day with different numbers (e.g., Nina 420, Caio 260).

With siblings

Print the mission pages once and solve them together, switching roles at each stage: reader (reads out loud), illustrator (draws the bar), and number cruncher (does the math and checks it against the drawing). With 2 children, the reader also draws. If they're at different levels, each one does the mission for their level and, at the end, they tell each other how they drew it.

Answer key: The Locked Safe Mystery

Stage 1 — Nina's points · 300 (letter G)
How to think: Before winning, she had 50 fewer: 350 − 50. Calculation: 350 − 50 = 300.
If they get stuck, ask: “Before the round, did Nina have more or less than 350?”
Most common mistake: answering 400, when your child added instead of going back to before the round. That answer leads to a different letter, and the word doesn't work out.
Stage 2 — The trade · 70 (letter A)
How to think: The difference is 140. To tie, half of the difference goes from one to the other. Calculation: 350 − 210 = 140 · 140 ÷ 2 = 70.
The expected drawing (any drawing with the same parts counts):
Before: the difference (140) is split in half; one piece stays with Nina and the other goes to Caio. After: the two bars are equal, with 280.
If they get stuck, ask: “If Nina gives 140, who ends up with more? And if she gives half?”
Most common mistake: answering 140, when your child gave the whole difference, and then Caio ended up with more. That answer leads to a different letter, and the word doesn't work out.
Stage 3 — After the trade · 280 (letter M)
How to think: Nina lost 70; Caio gained 70. Check both sides. Calculation: 350 − 70 = 280 (and 210 + 70 = 280).
If they get stuck, ask: “How much does Nina have now? And Caio? Is it equal?”
Most common mistake: answering 210, when your child forgot to add the 70 that Caio received. That answer leads to a different letter, and the word doesn't work out.
Stage 4 — The next round · 224 (letter E)
How to think: Find 1/5 of 280 (what was spent) and take it away from 280. Calculation: 280 ÷ 5 = 56 · 280 − 56 = 224.
If they get stuck, ask: “Is the question what was spent or what was left?”
Most common mistake: answering 56, when your child answered what was spent, not what was left. That answer leads to a different letter, and the word doesn't work out.
Code word: GAME. Nina gave 70 points and the two ended up with 280. The trade was fair and the GAME went on: in the next round, each one ended up with 224.
