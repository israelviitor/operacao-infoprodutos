# Bonuses — en-US (final, para os cards da LP)

**The Draw-It Card:** The Whole Method in 4 Words
Keep it on the table at homework time. When your child gets stuck, they glance at the card and remember: READ, DRAW, SOLVE, CHECK. You don't have to explain a thing.

**30 Lightning Challenges:** 5 Minutes a Day, No Screens
One mini-problem a day to draw and solve before dinner or in the car. In 30 days, drawing before solving becomes a habit, and "Do I add or multiply?" goes away.

**Mission Kit:** Agent Badge, Clue Sheet & Certificate
Your child gets their agent badge before the first mission and a certificate when they crack the case. It's the part that has your child asking to do math.

**Mastery Map:** Hang It on the Fridge
Every mission your child completes earns a mark on the map. They see their own progress, and so do you: proof they're improving, right there on the fridge.
