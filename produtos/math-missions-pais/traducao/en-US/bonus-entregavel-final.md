## Part D — 30 Lightning Challenges
[D.001] MATH MISSIONS
[D.002] Bonus
[D.003] 30 Lightning Challenges
[D.004] One mini-problem a day, in 5 minutes, to practice
[D.005] drawing before solving
[D.006] on days without a mission.
[D.007] How to use (for the adult)
[D.008] Print the sheets and, if you want, cut out one challenge per day. It fits in before dinner, in the car, or in the waiting room.
[D.009] Follow the order for your child's level:
[D.010] challenges 1 to 10 · Level 1
[D.011] 11 to 20 · Level 2
[D.012] 21 to 30 · Level 3
[D.013] . Start with challenge 1, even if your child is at Level 2 or 3: the first ones take 1 minute and lock in the drawing. If it gets easy, skip to the first challenge of their level.
[D.014] There's only one rule:
[D.015] first the drawing, then the math
[D.016] . If your child gets stuck, ask “What does the question want to know?” and “Show me that in a bar.”
[D.017] The answer key, with the expected drawing for each challenge, is on the last pages. Any drawing with the same parts counts.
[D.018] Challenge done? Your child colors in the day's circle on the Mastery Map.
